Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the Grammobolica mobile application for iOS and Android (the “App”), published on the App Store and Google Play as “Grammobolica” (formerly “Mnemolearn”). The App is developed and operated by the Grammobolica team (“we”, “us”, “our”). This Policy explains what data the App collects, why, who we share it with, and the choices you have.

The App has no user accounts: we do not ask for your name, email address, phone number or password to use it.

1) Data we collect

  • Learning settings. The languages you study and speak, your level, your interface language and, if you choose it, the grammatical gender used to address you in exercises. We use them to choose and form your exercises.
  • Learning progress. Topics, phrases and words you practise, your answers and review schedule. This is stored on your device; the parts needed to serve exercises (for example, which topic and level you request) are sent to our server.
  • Voice input. When you answer by voice, the App uses your device’s speech recognition service (Apple Speech on iOS, Google speech services on Android) to turn your speech into text. Audio is processed by that platform provider under its own privacy policy; the App receives only the recognized text and compares it with the expected answer. Recordings you make in pronunciation practice stay on your device. We do not upload or store your voice recordings on our servers. The App uses the microphone only after you grant permission and only while you are recording an answer.
  • Text you submit to AI features. If you use features that generate content (for example, building a word list from your text or a photo you pick), the text or image you submit is sent to our server and to OpenAI to generate the result. Photos are used only for that request; the App does not access your photo library without your action.
  • Text-to-speech requests. To play pronunciation, the text of the phrase is sent to our server and to Microsoft Azure Speech to synthesize audio. The audio may be cached to save repeat requests.
  • Usage and device data. Screens viewed and actions in the App (for example, a training started or finished), app version, device model, operating system, language, approximate country derived from your device settings or IP address, and time zone.
  • Crash and performance data. Error reports, stack traces and device state at the time of a crash.
  • Purchase data. Subscription status, product, price, currency, renewal and trial dates. Payments are processed by Apple or Google; we never see or store your card details.
  • Identifiers. A random app user ID generated by the App or our service providers, and a push notification token if you allow notifications.
  • Messages you send us. If you contact support or send a bug report from the App, we receive your email address, your message and anything you choose to attach (for example, a screenshot).

We do not collect your contacts, precise location, or the advertising identifier (IDFA / Android Advertising ID), and we do not use your data for advertising.

2) How we use data

  • To provide the App: deliver exercises, check your answers, play pronunciation, remember your progress, send the reminders you turn on.
  • To provide subscriptions: verify purchases, restore access, prevent abuse.
  • To keep the App working: diagnose crashes and errors, protect our server from abuse.
  • To improve the App: understand which features are used and where people get stuck.
  • To answer you when you contact us.

3) Who we share data with

We do not sell your data and do not share it with data brokers or advertisers. We use the following service providers, who process data on our behalf only to deliver the services below:

  • Google Firebase (Analytics, Crashlytics, Remote Config, Cloud Messaging, App Check) – analytics, crash reports, app configuration, push notifications, protection of our server.
  • Mixpanel – product analytics with a random app user ID.
  • Sentry – error and crash reports.
  • RevenueCat – subscription management.
  • Cloudflare – hosting of our server and content.
  • Microsoft Azure Speech – text-to-speech for phrase pronunciation.
  • OpenAI – processing of text and images you submit to AI features. Data sent through the OpenAI API is not used to train their models.
  • Apple and Google – app distribution, payments, speech recognition on your device’s platform, and push notification delivery, under their own privacy policies.

We may also disclose data if required by law or to protect the rights and safety of our users or us.

4) Data retention

Learning progress stays on your device until you delete the App or reset progress in it. Analytics and crash data are kept by our providers only for the limited retention period set for each service, and are then deleted or aggregated. Support emails are kept while needed to answer you. Purchase records are kept as long as required for accounting and legal obligations.

5) Your rights and choices

  • Delete your data: delete the App. There is no account, so deleting the App removes everything it stores on your device. Data held by our service providers is linked only to a random app ID that is gone with the App, so it can no longer be connected to you. Purchase records stay with Apple or Google in your store account.
  • Access, correction, objection. Depending on where you live (for example, under the GDPR or CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct or object to the processing of your data. Contact us at the address above.
  • Permissions. You can turn off microphone access, notifications and photo access at any time in your device settings; the App keeps working without voice answers, reminders or photo-based features.
  • Subscriptions. Manage or cancel in Apple settings or Google Play.

6) Legal bases (EEA/UK users)

We process data to perform our contract with you (providing the App and subscriptions), based on our legitimate interests (keeping the App secure and improving it), and with your consent where required (microphone, notifications, photos).

7) Children

The App is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

8) International transfers

Our service providers may process data on servers outside your country, including in the United States and the European Union. We rely on their safeguards, such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses, to protect it consistent with this Policy.

9) Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your data.

10) Changes

We may update this Policy. Changes will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date.
